I’ve built your practice app as a single file, index.html, with no installs and no server. I haven’t run it in a browser yet.

How it works

A sentence appears on screen. Press Listen to hear a model voice, and use the speed slider to slow it down.
Press Start speaking, read the sentence aloud, then press Stop.
The app compares what it heard to the sentence. Words you said clearly turn green, and missed or unclear words turn red and wavy.
It shows your accuracy %, your pace in words per minute (the comfortable range is about 110–160), and your best score on that sentence.
It gives short tips, such as which words to practise and whether you’re rushing or going too slowly.
There are four levels: warm-up sentences, tongue twisters, professional phrases and longer passages. You can also paste your own text to practise.
It tracks your attempts and daily streak, saved in your browser.
