    How to use
    1. Pick a situation in Reply practice, or a sound in Sound drills.
    2. Press Hear a model, then tap the mic and read the sentence aloud.
    3. Fix the amber, red and ⏸ marks, then try again until the sentence is clean.
    4. Use My text to practise your own words, and aim for 5 tries a day.
    5. In Elocution, pick a topic and speak for 30 seconds to 2 minutes. In Writing, write your answer and press Evaluate, fix the highlighted words, then evaluate again.
